About Me

I'm pursuing a B.S. in Information Technology at Clayton State University, with a concentration in Computer Engineering. I started with an Associate's in Computer Science from Southern Crescent Technical College.

I've been an Associate Banker at J.P. Morgan Chase since 2018. In 2026 I was a Technology Support Intern at the Clayton State University Tech Hub, where I imaged and deployed Windows machines, joined devices to the domain, repaired hardware, and staffed the help desk. My goal is to become an Electrical Engineer. Outside the classroom I do a lot of hands-on work diagnosing and repairing automotive electrical systems, including wiring, sensors, and other components. Tracing a fault back to its source is the part I enjoy most.

Away from the bench, I train in Jiu-Jitsu and Muay Thai, shoot pistol crossbow, and play cricket.
